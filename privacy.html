Orbillion privacy

Updated November 3, 2026

The short version

Orbillion collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your answers never leave your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: streak, scores, past climbs, minigame bests, Hangar unlocks and settings.
  • Every answer you type is checked on the device itself. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

iCloud (iPhone app)

If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, a copy of your progress is kept in your private iCloud storage so it comes back on a new phone. Apple stores it; the developer cannot see it. You can turn this off in the iPhone Settings app under your name, iCloud.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game hands a short text (your climb number, height and colored squares) to the share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick where it goes.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages and keeps your progress in your browser's own storage. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; see the GitHub privacy statement. Clearing your browser data clears your progress.

Children

Orbillion is made for all ages. It collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a note on the Orbillion support board, or see the support page.
