Questions

How do I play?

Each day has seven prompts like "Name a fruit." Type one answer per prompt before the timer runs out. Rarer answers burn hotter and lift Gus higher. A Moonshot is the rarest kind of answer.

When does the next climb open?

At midnight in your time zone. You can pick a different zone in Settings once a week; a zone can never open tomorrow's climb early.

How do streaks work?

Finish the daily climb on back to back days to grow your streak. Every 7 days in a row earns a Day Off (you can hold two), which saves your streak if you miss a single day.

Can I play more after the daily?

Yes. Open Games for Rapid Fire, Rarer?, Scramble, Free Flight and Past Climbs. They are just for fun and never touch your streak.

Why didn't my answer count?

Answers are checked against a list for each prompt. Small typos are fixed for you when the Spelling fixer is on. If you think a real answer is missing, let us know on the support board.

Will I lose my progress on a new phone?

On iPhone, your progress is kept in your iCloud account when iCloud is on, so it comes back when you sign in with the same Apple Account. On the web, progress lives in that browser.

Text is too small or too big

Settings has Small, Medium and Large text, plus options for sound, music, the announcer voice, vibration and reduce motion.
